A fork of Open Grind, the cross-platform, free, libre, ad-free, tracker-free,
privacy-centered and community-driven unofficial client for the Grindr queer
social network.

This fork started as an iOS port — the original builds for Android and desktop
— and grew into a redesign plus the features that were missing from daily use.
Most of the work in it was done with AI.

From Open Grind

• Unlocked grid
Unlimited profiles for free, ads removed from grid, all filters unlocked

• Location spoofing
Native geolocation change with OpenStreetMap and location search built-in

• Zero ads
No ads, first party, third party or self-promotion, no obstructive pop-ups

• Privacy by default
No analytics, zero trackers, no data is collected

• Security as foundation
Human code review, audit, supply chain security, end-to-end testing

Added in this fork

• Redesigned interface
A minimalist look throughout, redesigned settings, and your own theme and
colors

• Albums
View albums shared with you, create and edit your own, reorder photos, and
share or unshare from the chat composer, with an expiry if you want one

• Richer chat
Video messages and locations, replies to messages, photos and locations,
media downloads, and every photo from a conversation in one gallery

• Favorites
A favorites screen, with notes you can add to profiles and search through

• Gestures and feedback
Swipe between tabs and between profiles, haptics, and a bubble when someone
views your profile

• Blocked contacts
Manage everyone you have blocked
