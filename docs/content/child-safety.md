# Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 2026-09-16

Open Grind, made by Open Grind Governance, prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and trafficking of children. Open Grind is only for adults 18 and older.

Grindr hosts and moderates all profiles, photos and messages in Open Grind. [Grindr's Community Guidelines](https://www.grindr.com/community-guidelines) apply to them.

## Report abuse

- Report a profile or message to Grindr with Report in Open Grind, and choose Underage for anything involving a minor.
- Report anything on git.opengrind.org or in the Open Grind Matrix rooms to [admin@opengrind.org](mailto:admin@opengrind.org). Never send images of abuse to anyone, including us. Describe where you saw them instead.
